Who we are

Fifteen years of teaching students from our own neighbourhood

LearnSphere opened in 2011 with two tutors, one classroom and a simple promise: no student leaves a session still confused. Today we run more than forty weekly batches across Classes 1 to 12, and that promise has not changed.

We are deliberately small. Batches are capped, tutors teach only their specialist subjects, and every family gets a real person to talk to about progress.

  • Subject specialists with school teaching backgrounds
  • Batch sizes capped at eight students
  • Transparent monthly fees and no admission charges

Our mission

To give every school student in our neighbourhood access to patient, high-quality subject teaching at a fair monthly fee.

Our vision

A learning centre where students arrive nervous about a subject and leave able to teach it to someone else.

Our values

Honesty about progress, respect for every learner, and steady effort over shortcuts.

Our history

How the centre grew

  • 2011 — Two tutors, one roomLearnSphere opens on Maple Avenue with maths and science batches for Classes 6 to 10.
  • 2015 — Foundation programmeClasses 1 to 5 added, with a play-led numeracy and reading curriculum.
  • 2019 — Board-exam tracksDedicated Class 11–12 physics, chemistry and mathematics preparation launched.
  • 2023 — Student dashboardAttendance, materials and results moved online so parents can check progress any time.
  • 2026 — Forty weekly batchesForty-eight tutors now teach across eight subjects and four grade bands.
